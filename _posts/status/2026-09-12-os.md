Saturday update. For past few days, I was taking my shot and trying to install Linux, since everyone says "Windows shucks, glad I moved to Linux". I held onto my Windows 10 for so long because of too afraid of data loss (happened once). Initially I wanted dual boot although my boyfriend advised against it. Then, I was being stupid and ended up wiped the entire disk containing Windows... good riddance? So, plunging into Distro hopping frenzy. Always find new issue with each ones.

So like, the distros I tried were Fedora 44 KDE Plasma, Pop!_OS COSMIC, and... Nobara Official, which also KDE. The problem was: 1) I use CSP for work, even if I had succeed running with Bottles, it is completely different experience with stutters and less flexibility. 2) I have weird monitor setup, which I use both LG Monitor (supposedly using HDMI cable, but since my laptop only has one port so I bought VGA cable instead) and Huion Kamvas 13. Even when Huion driver successfully installed with .deb, it won't recognize the boundaries of screen, I can't use practical shortcut like pointer's position instant monitor switching. Also... audio crackling? Nobara was almost fine until I encountered that. In the end, I grabbed Windows 11 IoT LTSC from massgrave.dev.

Seriously, it feels like you've been dreaming about moving out your childhood house but then you realized about taking things for granted. Yeah Microslop is bloated, but the drivers are working fine (maybe because I use old ass Acer Aspire laptop with Hybrid Intel-NVIDIA thingy). My laptop runs smoother, thanks to new install. People can call me loser for going back to Windows, I don't care lmao. What's important, I learned so many things in short span of days.

WINTAB. STAY IN WINDOWS IF YOU NEED WINTAB.
